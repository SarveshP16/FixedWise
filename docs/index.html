Features

Everything an Indian FD needs — nothing it doesn't.

Track every deposit

  • All your family's FDs, tagged to holders — you, a parent, a spouse — and filterable by them.
  • A maturity ladder and a maturing soon list on the dashboard.
  • Reminders a few days before each FD matures.
  • Renew in one tap, or close early and record what the bank actually paid.

Calculated the way banks do

  • Cumulative or payout — monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annual.
  • Tenures in months and days: 444 days, 1 year 15 days, 7 days to 10 years.
  • Bank-accurate interest, including simple interest under 6 months and on leftover days.
  • Tax-saver (80C) FDs with the 5-year lock-in, nominees, and ₹12,34,567 formatting.

Tax & safety

  • Interest per financial year (April–March), per holder and per bank.
  • TDS estimate under Section 194A — ₹50,000 threshold, ₹1 lakh for senior citizens.
  • DICGC check: a warning when one person has more than ₹5 lakh at a single bank.

Private by design

  • No internet permission — no ads, trackers, account or server.
  • App lock with PIN or fingerprint; screenshots blocked while locked.
  • Automatic backups to a folder you choose, plus export and import.
  • Light and dark themes, following your system.
